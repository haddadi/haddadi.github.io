Hamed is the Professor of Human-Centred Systems at the Department of Computing at Imperial College London. In his industrial role, he is the Chief Scientist at Brave, and the Co-Founder at Mulini SRL. He is interested in better Utility, Privacy, and Security for AI Systems. He enjoys designing and building AI models and systems that enable better use of our digital footprint, while respecting users' privacy and provide verifiable security.


He studied for BEng/MSc/PhD at University College London. He was a postdoctoral researcher at Max Planck Institute for Software Systems in Germany, and a postdoctoral research fellow at Department of Pharmacology, University of Cambridge and The Royal Veterinary College, University of London. He has spent time working and collaborating with Arm, Intel Research, Microsoft Research, AT&T Research, Telefonica R&D, Huawei R&D, and Sony Europe. When not in the lab, he prefers to be on a ski slope or in a kayak.
